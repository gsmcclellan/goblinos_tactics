# Goblinos

A turn-based tactics game with roguelite elements — built in Godot 4 / C#.

---

## What is this?

You know how in every tactics game, goblins are the cannon fodder? The disposable little guys you mow through in chapter one before the real enemies show up?

**Goblinos is about being those guys.**

You command a squad of goblins — chaotic, expendable, and absolutely unhinged. Think Fire Emblem meets a roguelite, except instead of noble knights with tragic backstories, your roster is a rotating cast of little green idiots who will absolutely die. Combat is fast, tactical, and designed to be a little unhinged.

---

## Features (in development)

**Tactical combat**
- Grid-based, turn-based battles on maps with terrain, walls, and elevation
- Weapon triangle: Sword beats Axe beats Spear beats Sword. Hammers and guns laugh at armor.
- AOE attacks — cones, lines, blasts, cross patterns. Friendly fire is a design decision.

**Abilities**
- Push, swap, haste, heal, anchor (root), shield, stun, and more
- Status effects: bleed, poison, burn, blind, silence, lifesteal, reflect, thorns, cleave
- Environmental interactions (fire spreads, terrain reacts)

**Roguelite structure**
- Permadeath — your goblins are disposable by design
- Run-based progression
